Life Calendar: Measure Your Life in Weeks

We are used to measuring life in years. But try changing the perspective. On average, we have about 4,000 weeks. It doesn't seem infinite anymore, does it?

"Life Calendar" helps you realize the value of time. Enter your birthday, and you will see your entire life on one screen. Filled squares are your past, empty ones are your future.

Totally free, private and ad-free.

What you can do:
• Visualize your entire life at a glance and adjust your life expectancy
• Set goals for each week and attach photos and notes to keep memories
• Rate your weeks to analyze your life and see the big picture
• Get weekly reminders to reflect on what matters
• Back up your data to a file and restore it on another device
• Use the home screen widget to keep the calendar always visible

Quick gestures make navigation effortless: pull down to search weeks, pull up for the current week, and slide from the edge to open the menu.

Life is happening right now. Download the app and make every week count!
