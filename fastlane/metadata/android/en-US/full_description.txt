We are used to measuring life in years. But try changing the perspective. On average, we have about 4,000 weeks. It doesn't seem infinite anymore, does it?

"Life Calendar" helps you realize the value of time. Enter your birthday, and you will see your entire life on one screen. Filled squares are your past, empty ones are your future.

Totally Free & Private.

With this app, you can:
• Stop procrastinating and start living.
• Set goals and visualize your progress.
• Keep memories: attach photos and notes to any week.
• Analyze your life: rate your weeks to see the big picture.

Life is happening right now. Download the app and make every week count!
